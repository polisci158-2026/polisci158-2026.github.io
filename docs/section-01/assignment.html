Section Assignment 1

Make your personal website

Graded: Pass / No-Pass Due: before your Section 2 meeting (exact deadline on Canvas) Submit: one link on Canvas
Open the step-by-step guide

Why this assignment

You'll use an AI coding agent and GitHub throughout this course. This assignment sets up both tools on a small, low-stakes task you know well: yourself.

What to build

A one-page personal website, live at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io, with:

PartWhat it needs
AboutYour name and a few sentences about you in your own words
InterestsA few things you're interested in
Project interestsWhat kinds of projects you'd like to work on in this course: topics, questions, or the kind of work you enjoy. Classmates will use this to find group partners for the final project.
FooterOne line crediting the AI tool, e.g. "Built with Codex. Text written by Jordan Rivera."

You don't need a photo, a CV, contact details, or any coding knowledge. See the example site for the scale we have in mind.

To pass

  • Your URL opens your page, not a 404 or the README.
  • The page has the four parts above.
  • The words are yours, and they're true. The agent may structure and style the page, but it must not invent facts about you.

What to submit

One link

https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io

Before you submit, open the link in a private or incognito window to check that it works for everyone, not just you.

Privacy and access

  • Only publish what you're comfortable having online. Leave out your phone number, address, and student ID.
  • If you can't or don't want to host a public page, or if tool access or cost is a problem, contact your TA before the deadline.
